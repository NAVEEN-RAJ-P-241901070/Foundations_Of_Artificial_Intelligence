Explainable by construction

This prototype ports the PDF's rule evaluation into browser JavaScript. It uses no model, training data, server, or external library.

01 / Facts and rules

Each disease has weighted symptom facts and required premises. Only selected facts are present; explicit “No” answers are absent and other facts remain unknown.

02 / Forward rule evaluation

A single pass evaluates every disease rule. All required facts must be present before its implication fires. Ranking also includes partial overlaps; a partial match is not an inferred disease. 

score = 100 × matched weight / total weight
if any required fact is missing: score ×= 0.60

03 / Backward premise checks

Choose a hypothesis and inspect its required facts. Answer each unknown premise separately to recompute the results. An absent required premise blocks the rule.

04 / Consistency checks—not formal resolution

The source's contradiction pairs are fever / no fever, high fever / no fever, and diarrhea / constipation. Contradictory inputs block evaluation until corrected. This is a direct input consistency check, not a CNF resolution prover or first-order inference engine.

05 / Scope and safety

Required-premise matches are rule outputs, not clinically confirmed diagnoses. The toy rules can overlap and are neither exhaustive nor medically validated. The UI does not prescribe medication. A symptom-based safety notice is independent of the ranking and is not comprehensive triage.
